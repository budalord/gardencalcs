Watering Schedule Calculator

Get a personalized watering schedule based on your plant, soil, season, and growing method.

How to Use

  1. Select your plant type from the dropdown list.
  2. Choose your soil type — sandy soils drain faster and need more frequent watering.
  3. Select the current season to account for temperature and evaporation differences.
  4. Choose your growing method — containers dry out faster than in-ground beds.
  5. Click Calculate to get your personalized weekly watering schedule.
