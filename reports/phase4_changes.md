## Implementation summary

- Added a dedicated crop-data file (`config/seedSpacingCrops.ts`) instead of copying 15 mostly identical pages by hand.
- Added a dedicated route `app/tools/seed-spacing/[crop]/page.tsx` with `generateStaticParams()` for all 15 crop slugs.
- Kept the mother page at `/tools/seed-spacing-calculator` and rewired its internal-link section so it now exposes all 15 crop guides.
- Updated `app/sitemap.ts` so crop pages are generated by sitemap logic, not hand-appended XML.
- Kept crop pages lightweight: no calculator widget, no embed widget, no heavy client-only block above the fold.

## Notes

- The crop pages intentionally do **not** reuse the heavy generic `ToolLayout` shell. This keeps the first screen lightweight and makes the LCP element a server-rendered text paragraph instead of a client-side calculator block or embed.
- The mother page keeps the calculator, while the child pages act as crop-specific answer pages. That separation is what makes the cluster behave like a tool + guide system instead of 15 doorway clones.
