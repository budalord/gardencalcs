About

A reference shelf for gardeners, not a storefront.

We write free, source-cited garden calculators because every other place on the internet either asks you to log in, shows you ten ads before an answer, or makes the number up.

Every gardening question eventually comes down to a number — pounds of lime, cubic yards of compost, gallons per week, plants per row. The answer is out there, usually buried on a state University Extension PDF from 2004. We read those PDFs, we double-check them against two more, and we surface the math in a calculator that takes ten seconds.

What we actually are

A small editorial team. We make our living from the site's ad placements (non-intrusive; no pop-ups, no interstitials, no affiliate links baked into recommendations). We don't sell data. We don't have a newsletter that you'll regret signing up for — we don't have a newsletter at all.

Our principles

01

Cite or it didn't ship.

Every calculator links to the Extension publication it's derived from. If we can't source it, we don't publish it.

02

Free, no sign-up, no dark patterns.

No account required. No "unlock premium" modal. No newsletter pop-up 30 seconds after arrival. Ever.

03

Answer first, explain second.

The Quick Answer sits above the fold. The methodology, the formula, the sources — those are below, for the readers who want them.

04

Calibrated confidence.

When the Extension literature disagrees, we say so. When we're giving a rough estimate, we label it as one.

How we make a guide

Each calculator or guide follows the same pipeline:

  • Draft from primary sources. We start with at least three University Extension publications. Cornell CALS, NCSU Extension, UMN, Penn State, UC ANR, Clemson HGIC — these are the canon.
  • Cross-check numbers. Lime rates, spacing intervals, watering gallons — we verify each value appears consistently across sources before using it.
  • Worked example. Every calculator ships with at least one fully worked example, so you can verify the arithmetic by hand.
  • Citation footer. Every number in the result is traceable to a named publication in the sources list.
  • Review cycle. Every article carries a "last reviewed" date. We reread the canon on a 12-month cycle and update stale references.
Methodology note When a Cornell publication says "5 lb lime per 100 ft²" and a Penn State publication says "4 lb," we publish the range and explain the why (usually soil-texture assumptions). We don't pick one and pretend the other doesn't exist.

What we're not

  • Not a replacement for your local Extension service. Soil pH varies at the county level. If you need an actual soil test, send a sample to your state's Extension lab — they're underpriced and accurate.
  • Not a plant-diagnosis tool. If something is dying, call your county Master Gardener helpline. They're free.
  • Not an aggregator. Every piece of content is written here, against the primary sources, by an editor whose name is attached.

How we're funded

Unobtrusive display advertising — text-only or single-image sponsor blocks in the right rail on long pages. No affiliate links inside calculator results. No brand mentions in recommendations. If a calculator ever says "use Brand X," you have permission to email us about it — we haven't shipped one yet and don't plan to.

Contact

If you spot a math error, a wrong citation, or a stale fact — let us know. We fix errors within 48 hours and credit the reader in the page's revision footer.

1. What we collect

We collect as little as possible. Broken into three buckets:

Things that never leave your browser

Every calculator on the site runs entirely client-side. Your inputs — bed dimensions, pH readings, fertilizer analysis — are processed in your browser and are never transmitted to us. We have no way to see them, and neither does any third party.

Aggregate page-view counts

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused, cookie-less alternative to Google Analytics. It records aggregate page views, country (IP-derived, not stored), and referrer. It does not set cookies, does not fingerprint your browser, and does not follow you across sites. The data is anonymized at the point of collection.

Email, when you write to us

If you send us an email about a math error, a typo, or general feedback, we keep the thread until the issue is resolved. We delete it on request, and we purge inactive threads after 12 months.

2. Cookies

We set at most two first-party cookies, and only if you interact with a preference control:

  • gch_units — stores your preferred measurement system (ft/in vs m/cm). Lifetime 12 months.
  • gch_theme — stores your color-scheme preference, if you override the system default. Lifetime 12 months.

We do not set advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. We do not use any third-party cookies.

3. Advertising

The site is funded by display advertising. We serve static sponsor slots — text-only or single-image placements — that are rendered server-side. We do not use personalized ad networks. We do not pass your IP, user agent, or page content to advertisers beyond what the ad network receives as part of a standard HTTP request.

If we ever add a programmatic ad network, we will update this policy before switching the code, and we will not use any network that requires passing personal identifiers.

4. Third parties

We rely on the following third-party services to run the site:

  • Cloudflare — CDN and DDoS protection. Sees your IP during the request, does not persistently log it to us.
  • Plausible Analytics — aggregate page counts, as described above.
  • Font hosting — we self-host all web fonts. No Google Fonts calls.

We do not embed third-party video, social widgets, or chat scripts.

5. Your rights

Because we collect almost nothing, most data-rights frameworks don't materially apply to us. For completeness: you can email us to request access, correction, or deletion of any data we hold about you, including the content of your past correspondence. We'll respond within 30 days.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll bump the "Effective" date at the top of the page and keep a changelog. We don't do silent updates.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@gardencalcs.com. General inquiries: the contact form.

Before you write

I think a calculator is giving the wrong answer.

Send us: the page URL, the inputs you used, the value the calculator returned, and the value you expected with a source. We'll reproduce the bug and either fix the math or publish a note explaining the discrepancy.

Can I suggest a new calculator?

Yes — we keep a public wishlist. Calculators that already have strong primary-source literature (USDA, University Extension) move faster because the citations already exist. Tell us the query you'd type into Google, and we'll take it from there.

Can I reprint or syndicate your content?

Single-page reprint with credit and a canonical link back: yes, just email press@ for a nod. Full-site scraping or training-data use: no, please don't.

Do you take sponsorships?

We accept display advertising but not sponsored content. If a brand wants editorial mention, we decline — it's the one line we don't cross and a big part of why readers trust the calculators.

Are you hiring?

Occasionally — we look for editors with either an agronomy or Extension-volunteer background. If that's you, send a note to editors@ with a writing sample and a soil-pH opinion.
