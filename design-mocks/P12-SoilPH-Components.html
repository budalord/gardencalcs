Decision tree · "my pH test came back, now what?"

What the reading means

A four-zone interpretation for the most common target (6.5 for a general vegetable bed).

pH < 5.5
Strongly acidic — apply lime

Calcitic or dolomitic lime at 3–5 lb per 100 ft² for loam. Retest at 3 months. If still below target, apply a second pass in fall.

5.5 – 6.2
Mildly acidic — small dose

1–2 lb of lime per 100 ft² is usually enough to land at 6.5. Worth doing; not urgent.

6.2 – 6.8
In range — leave it alone

Amendments would overshoot. Retest in 2–3 years unless you see nutrient-deficiency symptoms.

6.8 – 7.5
Mildly alkaline — monitor

Most vegetables tolerate this. If you see interveinal yellowing, consider a light sulfur dose next fall.

pH > 7.5
Strongly alkaline — sulfur, patiently

Elemental sulfur at 1–2 lb per 100 ft². Split across two fall applications. For blueberries, consider raised beds with peat-based mix instead.

After a correction to 6.5, soil gradually reacidifies. The drift rate depends on climate, crop, and fertilizer program.
