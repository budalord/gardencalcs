Quick answer

For a 4×8 ft bed, 4 in deep

You need about 0.4 yd³ of finished compost — roughly three 14-gallon wheelbarrow loads.

Worth noting

Lime works slowly

Even at the right rate, dolomitic lime takes 3–6 months to fully react. Apply in fall for a spring-ready pH.

Watch out

Fresh manure is not compost

Uncomposted manure can burn roots and introduce pathogens. Age it at least 120 days before food-crop use.

For a 4 in topdress, about 0.4 yd³. For a brand-new fill to 12 in, about 1.2 yd³. Add 10% for settling in the first year.
Finished compost is usually near-neutral (pH 6.5–7.5) and buffers, rather than shifts, soil pH. To lower pH, use elemental sulfur.

Worked-example blockquote

A 4 × 8 ft bed at 4 inches deep holds 32 sq ft × 0.33 ft = 10.56 ft³. Divide by 27 to get cubic yards: 0.39 yd³. Round up to 0.4 yd³ and add 10% for settling — call it a half yard, delivered. Source: Rutgers NJAES FS119, "Calculating Mulch and Compost Volume" 1

Formula display

lime_lbs = (target_pH − current_pH) × area_sqft × factor

Where factor is 0.5 for sandy loam, 0.7 for loam, 1.0 for clay. Derived from Penn State AGRS-19.

§ 10 · Grid & rhythm

An 8-pixel base, three widths, measured gaps.

Everything sits on a multiple of 8. Body prose is capped at 640px for readable measure; calculator canvases open up to 1024px; full-bleed sections reach 1280px.

Shadows & depth

  • --shadow-* none — do not use

Rule: this system uses borders only. No box-shadow on any element. Depth is signalled by a 1px border in --ink-12 or a dashed --soil-40 rule. If a design ever seems to "need" a shadow, increase the border weight or introduce a --paper panel instead.

Motion

  • --motion-fast 150ms · hover, focus, accordion, toggles
  • --motion-tween 300ms · gauge sweeps, chart tweens, progress
  • --ease cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.6, 0.2, 1) — ease-out

Animate only color and transform. Never animate layout properties (width/height/top). Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Layout widths

  • --prose-max 640px · article body, FAQ prose
  • --canvas-max 1024px · calculator widget, tool page
  • --wide-max 1280px · homepage, section panels
