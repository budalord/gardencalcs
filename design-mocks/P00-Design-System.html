FAQ accordion
How often should I test soil pH? +
Test in-ground beds once per year, ideally in the fall after harvest so amendments have the winter to react. Raised beds with fresh compost should be tested every six months. Cornell Cooperative Extension · “Soil pH Testing” · 2022
Can I use wood ash to raise pH? +
Yes, but cautiously. Wood ash is roughly half as effective as agricultural lime and adds potassium — which can be undesirable at high rates. Apply no more than 20 lbs per 1,000 sqft per year. Oregon State Extension · EC 1503 · 2018
Does tap water change soil pH over time? +
Only if your municipal water is strongly alkaline (above pH 8.0). For most U.S. tap water, the effect is negligible over a single season. UGA Extension · B 1579 · 2020
Worked-example blockquote
A 100 sqft clay bed reading pH 5.5 needs approximately 4.7 lbs of agricultural lime to reach pH 6.5 — applied in the fall and tilled in to 6 inches.
Worked example · Cornell Extension (2022)
Formula display
lime_lbs = (target_pH − current_pH) × area_sqft × factor
factor = { sandy: 0.03, loamy: 0.05, clay: 0.08 }
