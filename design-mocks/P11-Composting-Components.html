Pile layering diagram

How to build the pile

Alternate layers roughly 4 inches thick. A 3 × 3 × 3 ft pile is the minimum for it to heat up on its own.

  1. Base layer · coarse brownsTwigs or straw, 4 in thick, for airflow.
  2. First greensKitchen scraps, grass — 4 in.
  3. Cover with brownsLeaves or shredded paper — 4 in.
  4. MoistenWring-out-a-sponge damp, never soggy.
  5. RepeatAlternate to 3 ft tall; cap with browns.

What the pile should be doing

A healthy hot pile rises above 130 °F within a week, holds for 2–3 weeks, then falls as it cures.

8-week composting checklist

Print or check off as you go. State is local to this page for the demo.

  • Build the pile — alternate browns and greens to 3 ft tall.Week 1
  • First temperature check — target ≥ 130 °F.Week 2
  • First turn — outside material to the center.Week 3
  • Second turn — moisten if dry; cover with browns.Week 4
  • Temperature drop expected — 100–120 °F is normal.Week 5
  • Third turn — pile volume should be half original.Week 6
  • Curing phase — stop turning, let mature.Week 7
  • Finished — crumbly, earthy smell, no recognizable scraps.Week 8
